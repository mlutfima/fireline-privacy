Privacy Policy — FireLine 521

Last updated: 13 August 2026
Summary: FireLine 521 collects no personal data. All calculations run locally on your device. There is no account, no login, no internet connection required, and no analytics or advertising.

What data we collect

None. FireLine 521 does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics. The app performs all engineering calculations locally on your device using the values you enter (pipe size, pressure, temperature, gas composition, and related inputs).

PDF report feature

The app can generate a PDF report summarising your calculation. This document is created locally on your device. It is not uploaded to any server. If you choose to share it, the file is passed to your device's own share function (for example, email or a messaging app) — the choice of recipient and method is entirely yours, and FireLine 521 has no access to or record of what you share or with whom.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location. No account or sign-in is required to use any feature.

Third-party services

FireLine 521 does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

FireLine 521 is an engineering screening tool intended for professional and technical use and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a revised "Last updated" date.
